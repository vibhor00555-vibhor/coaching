FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Triton Learning Academy.

What is included in the Free Demo & Consultation?

During your 20–30 minute session, our academic coordinator evaluates the student's current proficiency, discusses upcoming academic hurdles (such as AP exams or grade targets), and demonstrates our live interactive platform.

What time zones do you cater to?

We accommodate all US & international time zones (Pacific, Mountain, Central, Eastern, GMT, and IST). Tutoring sessions are scheduled during after-school hours, evenings, and weekends.

How are tutoring sessions conducted?

All sessions take place live 1-on-1 via Google Meet / Zoom with digital interactive whiteboards, screen sharing, real-time problem-solving, and downloadable lecture notes.

What is your rescheduling policy?

We know busy student schedules change. You can easily reschedule any upcoming session with at least 12–24 hours advance notice directly with your dedicated tutor.
